The pile is real. The list is not the work.

The Fix and Scale diagnostic scores eight things every owner-operated business can pin. Most rooms tick several. A lot tick all of them. Trying to fix the pile is how you stay stuck.

  • DemandTop of funnel. Not enough customers. One channel dying would quiet the phone.
  • PricePrices are soft. Scared to raise. Competing on cheap.
  • DeliveryEvery job is a fire. You are the operations manual.
  • MarginCashflow, reserves, profit. You fly by the bank balance.
  • Owner DependenceNothing moves without you. Two weeks off would stall it.
  • Sales SystemLeads come in and disappear. Close is a vibe, not a number.
  • Team AccountabilityWrong seats, soft culture. You are the manager and the consequence.
  • Operating RhythmNo cadence. Cannot grow without breaking. The week runs you.
